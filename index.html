I'm a naturally curious person, drawn to understanding things rather than simply experiencing them at face value. I've always had a fascination with the world in its many dimensions; its history, its people, its ideas, its contradictions, and the countless ways in which we interpret and shape our existence.

I enjoy exploring different disciplines, not because I struggle to settle on one, but because I find value in understanding how different fields connect. Music, philosophy, history, technology, literature, and physical training may seem like separate pursuits, but to me they share something fundamental: each demands curiosity, patience, discipline, and a willingness to go beyond the obvious. The subject may change, but the standard of commitment does not.

Creativity has always been an important part of who I am. Music and technology have played a particularly significant role in my life, both personally and professionally, while writing, reading, and documenting my experiences give me other ways to express myself and make sense of the world. I enjoy travelling, discovering unfamiliar places, observing different cultures, and collecting experiences along the way. I'm someone who appreciates the details: a conversation, a place, a piece of music, an idea, or a passing moment that might otherwise go unnoticed.

I'm equally drawn to the intellectual and the practical. I can spend hours exploring an idea, tracing its origins, questioning its assumptions, and considering its implications. But I also believe that understanding means very little without application. Discipline, consistency, and personal responsibility matter to me. Whether I'm developing a skill, building something, studying a subject, or working towards a physical goal, I value the process of gradual improvement over the satisfaction of temporary achievement.

I have a particular appreciation for history, theology, philosophy, ancient traditions, symbolism, and the evolution of human thought. I'm interested in how people think, why societies develop the way they do, how beliefs shape behaviour, and how the systems we create ultimately shape us in return. I enjoy questioning established perspectives, but equally value the humility required to recognise when I might be wrong. Being willing to reconsider an idea is, in my view, just as important as having the conviction to defend one.

Outside of all that, I don't believe life should be reduced to productivity, professional achievements, or the pursuit of material success. Ambition has its place, but so does music played simply for the pleasure of it, a good book, a long conversation, travelling somewhere unfamiliar, spending time with people who matter, or taking a moment to appreciate where you are. Not everything meaningful needs to serve a greater objective.

Ultimately, I'm someone who wants to keep learning, creating, experiencing, and becoming. I value depth over appearances, substance over pretence, and genuine curiosity over the illusion of knowing everything. I don't expect to have life figured out, nor do I particularly wish to. I'd rather remain open to discovery, challenge my own assumptions, and continue finding new reasons to be interested in the world.

Professional Profile

My professional background spans entrepreneurship, product management, business development, marketing, technology, and operations. I've worked across several industries, from music and entertainment to e-commerce, blockchain, and decentralised finance, taking on roles that have required both strategic thinking and hands-on execution.

I'm particularly drawn to building things: developing products, identifying opportunities, bringing people together, establishing partnerships, and turning ideas into functioning businesses. Throughout my career, I've worked at the intersection of commercial strategy and technology, often operating across multiple disciplines to move projects from concept to execution.

Selected Achievements

Marketing, Promotion & Entertainment

Founded and independently managed a music project, overseeing its commercial development, promotion, booking, and operations. Organised approximately 120–150 live performances annually for my own band over an eight-year period, in addition to organising and supporting numerous shows for other bands. Performed alongside internationally recognised acts including Opeth, Behemoth, Wardruna, Septicflesh, Carach Angren, Eluveitie, and many others, and appeared at major metal festivals. Oversaw extensive merchandise and distribution operations, including more than 20,000 CDs distributed in under a year, helping establish the project within the international underground metal scene.

E-commerce, Product Management & International Retail

Managed product development, operations, and team coordination at Wulflund, an international historical and alternative merchandise retailer. Responsibilities included product lifecycle management, local production, sourcing, inventory, wholesale, customer relations, and supplier negotiations. Took primary responsibility for merchandise and wholesale operations connected to major entertainment productions and franchises, including Game of Thrones (TV series), Vikings (TV series), and The Witcher (video game and television series), among others. Also contributed to establishing the company's Etsy operation, which generated more than $1 million in net profit during its first year.

Pioneering Real-World Marketing in DeFi

I was the first to take a DeFi project into large-scale traditional outdoor advertising through a campaign that, at the time, was virtually unheard of in the industry. While crypto promotion was overwhelmingly concentrated around influencers, online communities, and digital advertising, I recognised the potential of bringing DeFi brands directly into the physical world.

I conceived and executed a large-scale billboard campaign spanning the United States and Canada, securing prominent placements in major locations. One of its centrepieces was a placement on the largest advertising screen in New York's Times Square. Rather than simply purchasing advertising space, I developed a campaign around the visibility and social reach of the placement itself, arranging for people to visit the location, photograph themselves with the advertisement, and share those images. This turned a traditional billboard placement into a participatory marketing campaign with an additional online reach.

The campaign coincided with the project reaching a market capitalisation of approximately $100 million in a remarkably short period. Its success attracted attention from major crypto projects, including SafeMoon and Shiba Inu, as well as other established projects that preferred to remain anonymous. Their teams reached out to learn how the campaign had been conceived and executed, and to explore implementing similar strategies for their own projects.

It was an early example of taking DeFi marketing beyond the industry's established digital channels and applying the reach, visibility, and mechanics of mainstream advertising to a blockchain-native project.

The Beckley Foundation — CURE Token

Worked with The Beckley Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit philanthropic organisation, on the development of CURE Token, a blockchain-based charitable initiative supporting families in the United States affected by childhood cancer.

The initiative incorporated a fundraising campaign that included a Hellcat giveaway, using the CURE token and its surrounding community to bring attention and support to the cause. The Beckley Foundation has raised more than $3 million overall through its charitable work.

Blockchain, Business Development & Advisory

Through Progressus Advisory, Octave Advisory in the UAE, and other ventures, worked with more than 100 projects across the blockchain and Web3 ecosystem. Involved in business and product development, fundraising, strategic partnerships, blockchain platforms, and smart contract initiatives. Also served in Chief Technology Officer (CTO) and managerial roles, providing technical leadership, managing people, and coordinating teams across projects and business operations.

One of the most significant milestones was pioneering the legal infrastructure for tokenised equity. I was the first to secure the legal registration of a company specifically structured to allow equity to be tokenised and represented within the crypto ecosystem, creating a legally established route for bringing traditional equity into blockchain-based markets.

I also contributed to the development of early launchpad ecosystems, including projects that went on to reach reported market capitalisations exceeding $100 million, alongside other initiatives involving tokenisation and early legally structured IDO frameworks.

During this period, I also worked closely with Sergey Sholom for approximately two years. Recognised for independently raising $50 million, he brought an exceptional level of experience in fundraising, deal-making, and capital formation. Working alongside him provided invaluable exposure to high-level business strategy, investor relations, negotiation, and the practical realities of raising substantial capital. It was an experience that significantly broadened my understanding of business and investment.

Blockchain Infrastructure & Ecosystem Development

Participated in the development and growth of early DeFi ecosystems, launchpads, and blockchain-based projects, working across smart contract infrastructure, token mechanics, and decentralised applications.

One initiative I took particular ownership of was the creation of TON's official burn address mechanism. At the time, TON was the only functioning blockchain I encountered without an established burn address. I identified this gap and took the initiative to create a recognised mechanism for permanently removing tokens from circulation.

The intention was to provide infrastructure that could serve the wider TON ecosystem, allowing projects, developers, and communities to send tokens to a recognised burn address, implement token-burning mechanisms, and build projects around those mechanics, including the emerging meme-token ecosystem.

I also developed a fully operational automated trading bot on TON and worked alongside InterstellarDAO on projects during the early expansion of the ecosystem, helping drive initiatives that reportedly reached market capitalisations in the tens of millions.

Automation, Growth Systems & Strategic Partnerships

Designed and built an automated Telegram-based acquisition and affiliate system for a prominent YouTube creator. This was far more than a conventional bot: I developed the complete acquisition flow, platform, backend infrastructure, and operational system that moved users from private communities into trading funnels and private groups.

The system generated approximately $3 million in profit through its affiliate model in a remarkably short period. Its success attracted attention from other prominent operators in the space, including Mauric Neo, Luke Belmar and others, who reached out to me to understand and explore the model.

The infrastructure and operating concept also became a precursor to the approach behind Luke Belmar's GemsHunters. The acquisition mechanics, user flow, platform structure, and backend operations originated in the system I had built.

At the time, this end-to-end approach to automated acquisition, affiliate monetisation, and private-community onboarding was unprecedented in the blockchain space. The work also led to partnerships and advisory relationships with major Web3 applications and trading platforms, including BullX and many others.

CryptoData — Alpha Circle

Joined the original team behind CryptoData's Alpha Circle, contributing to product development, growth strategy, operations, marketing, and community development. Helped shape the group's educational direction, market philosophy, and operational structure, contributing to its growth from inception into one of the prominent private trading communities in its segment. The community reportedly reached seven-figure scale within its first year.

Alongside the operational and strategic work, I also contributed as an educator, covering trading psychology, emotional discipline, behavioural patterns, and trading methodologies.

Beyond the Milestones

What you've read here represents only a selection of the milestones I've reached along the way, not the entirety of the journey. And while a collection of achievements can paint an impressive picture, it rarely tells the whole story. Behind every accomplishment are countless hours of work, difficult decisions, uncertain beginnings, sleepless nights, wrong turns, failed experiments, and lessons learned the hard way. There have been more setbacks than successes, more moments of doubt than certainty, and more occasions where things had to be rebuilt from the ground up than any polished profile could ever convey.

I've learned that progress is rarely as orderly as it appears in retrospect. Some of the most valuable lessons came from things that didn't work, opportunities that slipped away, and ideas that took far longer to realise than expected. Every experience, whether rewarding or humbling, has contributed something to the person and professional I've become.

I don't share these milestones to suggest that the journey has been extraordinary or that I have everything figured out. I share them because they represent years of curiosity, persistence, experimentation, and a genuine willingness to build something from nothing. The achievements are worth acknowledging, but so is everything that happened beyond the frame: because that is where much of the real work, and much of the growth, took place.
